Why a hardware Hamiltonian library?

Algorithms in quantum optimal control are often tested only on two-level or toy spin models. Each entry here gives a literature-grounded model Hamiltonian with realistic scales, constraints and starter code in QuTiP and Julia, so new optimizers can be benchmarked across real architectures. Missing a platform (e.g. photonics, ultracold lattices, molecular rotors)? Please open an issue on GitHub.
