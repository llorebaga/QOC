About this list

The list starts with well-known groups and grows over time. An entry needs a website that names its PIs; the location is the institution's entry on Wikidata, and the topics are the most frequent ones among the PIs' tracked papers (so they reflect recent output, not a self-description). Missing a group, or found an error? Please open an issue on GitHub.
