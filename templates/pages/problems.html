How problems are chosen

A problem is listed only if it can be stated precisely and a published paper poses it — as a conjecture, an explicit open question or a stated goal. Its status follows the literature: partial progress means that special cases are proved, solved that the full statement is. Solved problems stay in the list with the paper that solved them. Know of a result we missed, or a problem that belongs here? Please open an issue on GitHub.
