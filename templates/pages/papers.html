How this list is made

Every day, new arXiv papers are matched against a published selection rule and assigned up to four topics of the field map by a phrase-based classifier (data/classifier.yaml). Checked against hand-labelled papers, about {{ "{:.0%}".format(quality.topics.precision) }} of the assigned topics are correct and {{ "{:.0%}".format(quality.topics.recall) }} of the right ones are found. Topics are a guide for browsing, not a judgement of the papers.
